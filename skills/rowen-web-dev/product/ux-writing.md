# UX Writing

Treat interface words as part of interaction design.

- Use plain, specific verbs.
- Name actions by what happens: "Save changes", not "Submit".
- Keep the same term throughout a flow.
- Let each piece of copy do one job.
- Error text says what happened and, when possible, how to recover.
- Empty states explain the next useful action.
- Success messages confirm the actual result.
- Avoid filler, fake warmth and corporate AI language.
- Do not invent product capabilities, guarantees, metrics or legal claims.

Use HumanScope principles for natural writing, but prioritize clarity over stylistic novelty inside product UI.
